The Genesis

The Story Behind Plant Green Inertia

Mr. Laven Lokesh Barani is a visionary trainer, entrepreneur, and youth mentor with over eight years of impactful experience in higher education, skill development, and grassroots entrepreneurship.

Established in 2017, Plant Green Inertia (PGI) was founded with a singular conviction: that India's educated youth possess immense potential, but standard curricula lack experiential immersion. PGI earned recognition under the DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Govt. of India) and quickly became the official state-level training partner for the Government of Tamil Nadu's Naan Mudhalvan scheme.

"Every student deserves a bridge between classroom learning and real-world experience."
Statewide Footprint

Empowering Over 5 Lakhs Students Across Tamil Nadu

With an unrelenting passion for student upliftment, Mr. Laven has personally conducted over 1,000 workshops, experiential bootcamps, and career readiness seminars across 110+ collegiate campuses.

His sessions focus on direct practical learning, algorithmic thinking, executive communication, and real-world software architecture. Through high-energy, memorable pedagogy, he has inspired countless first-generation graduates to secure positions at premier corporate firms and IT enterprises.

  • Recognized by TNSDC & Directorate of Collegiate Education
Incubation & Wealth Creation

Mentoring 100 Next-Generation Entrepreneurs

Beyond student training, Mr. Laven has committed to mentoring 100 aspiring student entrepreneurs. His mission is to guide high-aptitude student innovators through ideation, corporate structure, product-market fit, and capital strategy.

Through rigorous hands-on incubation, market exposure, and institutional backing, this initiative is engineered to help these 100 student-led ventures attain a collective net worth of ₹100 Crore.

The 2 Million Student Vision

By expanding PGI's digital LMS and experiential internship modules, Mr. Laven aims to empower 2,000,000 learners over the coming years.
